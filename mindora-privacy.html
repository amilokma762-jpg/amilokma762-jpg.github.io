MINDORA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

MINDORA is a focus app for iPhone. This page explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Mohammed Daghbar publishes MINDORA. Contact: amilokma762@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Finished focus sessions, achievement unlock dates, your streak and your daily goal.
  • Your settings: appearance, language, reminders and default session length.
  • The apps and categories you choose for blocking and limits. Apple gives MINDORA opaque tokens, not app names, and the tokens never leave your iPhone.
  • Focus minutes and streak, shared with MINDORA's widget and Lock Screen timer through an app group on your iPhone.
  • A daily count of how many times you chose "Not now" on the blocked-app screen. It is kept in the same app group, never leaves your iPhone, and resets each day.

MINDORA has no account system and no cloud database. We do not receive this information.

Screen Time

With your permission, MINDORA uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity) to show a usage report, block apps during focus sessions, and enforce a daily limit. Apple produces the usage report on your iPhone. MINDORA does not read or upload your usage numbers or the names of your apps. You can turn this off at any time in iOS Settings > Screen Time.

Notifications

If you turn them on, MINDORA schedules local notifications for session completion, a daily reminder and a streak reminder. They are created on your iPhone and are not sent through a server.

Advertising

MINDORA is free and shows ads with Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect and use device and advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about how ads perform, as described in Google's advertising policy and Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it, MINDORA asks for your consent first through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice later in Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices.

Your focus history, Screen Time information, chosen apps, and statistics are never shared with Google or used to target ads. MINDORA does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use your Advertising Identifier to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Children

MINDORA is meant for everyone, but it is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and we have no account system or server that stores it. Ads shown in MINDORA are limited to a general-audience content rating. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has given personal information through the ads shown in the app, contact us at the address above and we will help.

Data we keep, and your rights

We do not run servers for MINDORA and we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf. Everything described under "What stays on your iPhone" can be removed by you. Data that Google collects for ads is handled by Google under its own policy; where your local law gives you rights over that data (for example access or deletion), you can exercise them with Google.

Your choices

  • Delete your focus history and achievements: Settings > Privacy > Reset local progress.
  • Remove everything: delete the app.
  • Change ad privacy choices where offered: Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy choices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
