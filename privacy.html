1. Who we are

"New Year 2027" (the "app") is an iPhone app published by MOHAMMED DAGHBAR ("we", "us"). Contact: amilokma762@gmail.com.

2. Summary

The app needs no account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location, and we do not run a server that collects data about you. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and Google and its partners may process some technical data to do so (section 4).

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the app stores on your device: the language you selected, your favorite cards, wishes and quotes, the card templates you unlocked, and your best score in the game. This information stays on your device, we do not receive it, and it is removed when you delete the app.

4. Advertising

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner ads, occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch. When ads are shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the advertising identifier if you allow tracking), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, ad interactions, and app and device diagnostics. They use this data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you agree, to personalize ads. How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Your choices about ads

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before showing ads. You can review or change your choice at any time in the app: Settings › Privacy & ad choices.
  • Tracking permission (iOS): the app may ask permission to track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you decline, ads still appear but may be less relevant.
  • Personalized ads: you can also manage ad personalization in your Google account at adssettings.google.com.

6. Sharing and photos

When you choose "Save & Share", the app creates the card as an image on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. We do not receive the card or the text you typed. The app cannot read your photo library; iOS may ask your permission to add the image to Photos if you choose to save it there. We do not choose recipients or send anything on your behalf.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law sets a higher age). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about advertising data are best made to Google (see section 4 and section 5) or by contacting us at the address above. We do not sell your personal information in exchange for money. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Retention and international transfers

Data stored on your device remains until you delete the app. Google and its partners keep advertising data under their own policies and may process it in countries other than yours, with the safeguards described in those policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the law changes. The effective date above shows the latest version, and the version inside the app (Settings › Privacy Policy) is kept in line with this page.

11. Contact
